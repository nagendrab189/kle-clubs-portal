How it works

Three easy steps to join your favourite club.

1

Open Club Webpage

Click any club card to read about the club, its advantages, and member perks.

2

Register & Attend Offline Test

Use the registration link inside the club's Offline Test section and attend the audition/test.

3

Pass & Join Club

Once you pass the offline test, the Club Head adds you to the official club members list.
